    Race log

      How to play

      1. Grid & momentum. Every car sits on a grid point and has a velocity — the step it made last turn, drawn as a dashed arrow.
      2. Your move. Your car repeats its last step (to the end of the dashed arrow), and you may shift that end point by one grid step in any direction — or not at all. That gives the 9 points shown on the board. The step you take becomes your new velocity.
      3. Choosing. Click or tap a point, use the move pad, or press the matching key: numpad 1–9, or Q W E / A S D / Z X C (the centre key keeps your speed). On touch screens, tap a point once to select it and again to confirm.
      4. Crashes. A move whose straight path touches a wall (even a corner), leaves the track, or ends on another car is a crash: your turn ends, your car stays where it was, and its speed drops to zero. Crash points are marked ✕; the game asks you to confirm before you pick one.
      5. Winning. The first car to cross the checkered finish line in the direction of the arrow — after the set number of laps — wins. Depending on the Race ends setting, the race stops right there or the others race on for the remaining places. Reversing over the line costs you a lap.
      6. Tip. You can only brake by one unit per turn in each direction, so plan your braking well before the bends.

      Online play

      Everyone sees the same race, kept in sync by the server. If a turn timer is set and runs out, an autopilot moves for you. If you lose your connection, the game waits a little for you to come back and then the autopilot keeps your car going until you reconnect.
